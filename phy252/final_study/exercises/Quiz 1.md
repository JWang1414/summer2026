# Question 3
---
a.
Assume the box is perfectly insulated.

Energy in the pendulum:
$$
	mgh = 0.1(9.8)(1) = 0.98
$$
From TD1:
$$
	0=dE=dW+dQ \implies dW=-dQ
$$
$$
	\Delta Q=C\Delta T \implies \Delta T = \frac{\Delta Q}{C}
$$
Change in temperature is:
$$
	\Delta T = \frac{\Delta W}{(5 /2)Nk_{B}}
$$
The number of particles is:
$$
	PV=Nk_{B}T \implies N = \frac{PV}{k_{B}T}
$$
$$
	\frac{5}{2}Nk_{B} = \frac{5}{2} \left( \frac{PV}{k_{B}T} \right)k_{B} = \frac{5}{2} \frac{PV}{T}
$$
So the change in temperature is:
$$
	\Delta T = \frac{2T_{i}(\Delta W)}{5PV}
$$
Where $T_{i}$ is the initial temperature. $P$ is pressure, $V$ is volume, and $\Delta W$ is the work done by the pendulum.

$$
	PV=Nk_{B}T \implies P = \frac{Nk_{B}T}{V}
$$
