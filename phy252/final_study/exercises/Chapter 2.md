# Question 10
Temperature is defined to be:
$$
	T \equiv  \frac{2}{3k_{B}}\left< E_{K} \right>
$$

$$
	\frac{3}{2}k_{B}T = 6.21\times 10^{-20}
$$
The pressure inside from the ideal gas law is:
$$
	P\left< V \right> = Nk_{B}T \implies P = \frac{Nk_{B}T}{\left< V \right> } = \frac{10^{23}k_{B}(3000)}{0.001} \approx 4140000
