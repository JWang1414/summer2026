# Question 7
Assume the particles do not interact with each other.
$$
	v=\frac{\Delta d}{\Delta t} \implies \frac{1}{\Delta t} = \frac{v}{\Delta d} = \frac{300}{0.1} = 3000
$$
So about 3000 times per second.
# Question 8
Surface area of a sphere is: $4\pi r^{2}$. So the total surface area of the atoms are:
$$
	N(4\pi a^{2}) = 1257
$$
The box, on the other hand, has a surface area of:
$$
	6l^{2} = 0.06
$$
Both are expressed in metres squared. So the atoms are ~21000 times more likely to hit an atom instead of a wall inside of this box.

However, by volume, the atoms take up substantially less space.
# Question 10
Temperature is defined to be:
$$
	T \equiv  \frac{2}{3k_{B}}\left< E_{K} \right>
$$
Average energy inside is therefore:
$$
	\frac{3}{2}k_{B}T = 6.21\times 10^{-20}
$$
The pressure inside from the ideal gas law is:
$$
	P\left< V \right> = Nk_{B}T \implies P = \frac{Nk_{B}T}{\left< V \right> } = \frac{10^{23}k_{B}(3000)}{0.001} \approx 4140000
$$
The pressure inside is 4.14 MPa.
