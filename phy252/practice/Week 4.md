# Question 1
---
b.
Ideal gas law:
$$
	PV=Nk_{B}T
$$
Initial temperature is:
$$
	T_{1} = \frac{P_{1}V_{1}}{Nk_{B}}
$$
At the end of step A the volume has doubled, and so the temperature has also doubled.
$$
	T_{2}=2T_{1}
$$
---
c.
Work:
$$
	W = -\int P \, dV = -P_{1}(V_{2}-V_{1}) = P_{1}(V_{1}-V_{2}) = P_{1}(V_{1} - 2V_{1})
$$
$$
	W = -P_{1}V_{1}
$$
Constant pressure heat capacity:
$$
	C_{P} = C_{V} + Nk_{B} = \frac{5}{2}Nk_{B} + Nk_{B} = \frac{7}{2}Nk_{B}
$$
Heat:
$$
	\Delta Q = C(\Delta T) = \frac{7}{2}Nk_{B} (T_{2}-T_{1}) = \frac{7}{2}Nk_{B} (2T_{1}-T_{1}) = \frac{7}{2}Nk_{B}T_{1}
$$
$$
	\Delta Q = \frac{7}{2}P_{1}V_{1}
$$
---
d.
Step B. Isothermal expansion.
$$
	W = Nk_{B}T_{3} \log\left( \frac{V_{2}}{V_{3}} \right) = Nk_{B}(2T_{1}) \log\left( \frac{V_{2}}{2V_{2}} \right) = 2Nk_{B}T_{1}\log\left( \frac{1}{2} \right) = -2Nk_{B}T_{1}\log 2
$$
$$
	Q = -W = 2Nk_{B}T_{1}\log 2
$$
Step C. Constant pressure compression.
$$
	W = -P(\Delta V) = -P_{2}(V_{4}-V_{3}) = -\frac{P_{1}}{2}\left( \frac{V_{3}}{2} - V_{3} \right) = \frac{1}{4}P_{1}V_{3} = P_{1}V_{1}
$$
Step D. Isothermal compression.
$$
	W = Nk_{B}T \log\left( \frac{V_{i}}{V_{f}} \right)= Nk_{B}T_{1} \log\left( \frac{V_{4}}{V_{1}} \right) = Nk_{B}T_{1} \log 2
$$
The net work is:
$$
	W_\text{net} = Nk_{B}T_{1}\log 2 - 2Nk_{B}T_{1} \log 2 = -Nk_{B}T_{1}\log 2 = -P_{1}V_{1}\log 2
$$
The heat input is:
$$
	Q_\text{in} = \frac{7}{2} Nk_{B}T_{1} + 2Nk_{B}T_{1} \log 2 = \left( \frac{7}{2} + 2 \log 2 \right)P_{1}V_{1}
$$
The efficiency of this heat engine is:
$$
	\frac{P_{1}V_{1}\log 2}{\left( \frac{7}{2}+2 \log 2 \right)P_{1}V_{1}} = \frac{2\log 2}{7+4\log 2} \approx 0.14
$$
This is less efficient than the Carnot engine.

	P = \frac{dW}{dt}

The work done from one cycle is:
