Introduction to the connection between entropy and the surprise:
$$
	\frac{S}{k_{B}} = \left< \mathcal{S} \right>
$$
Where $S$ is the entropy, and $\left< \mathcal{S} \right>$ is the average surprise in the system.

Generally speaking, if we do not have a full understanding of a system, it is useful to try and maximize the surprise. It is the sane way of approaching the problem (as opposed to trying to guess a specific outcome). With this new relationship, we can see that maximizing the surprise is equivalent to maximizing $S$. Maximum $S$ is the definition of thermal equilibrium.
$$
	\text{Max} \left< \mathcal{S} \right> = \text{Max } S \equiv  \text{Equilibrium}
$$
